One exact contract.

The evaluator starts with exactly $10. You do not deposit or top up money, and the bot cannot borrow or use leverage.
✓
Spend no more than $5 cash on any BUY, including protocol and platform fees.
✓
Reach at least $1,000 in eligible executed volume within the 24-hour official run.
✓
At the first $1,000 completed-cycle crossing, have zero positions and no pending actions. There is no separate minimum ending-balance rule.
✓
Hold at most one position or pending BUY at a time.
✓
Use YeNo BTC five-minute Up/Down markets only.
✓
Return valid decisions and reproduce from the submitted immutable commit or container digest.
✓
Never self-trade, coordinate fills, or trade against another competition bot or entrant-controlled account.
✓
Submit only your algorithm. The competition does not give you a trading account or place real orders for you.
Scoring

Complete cycles create volume. Nothing else does.
The bot cannot inflate its score with retries, order count, or unfinished inventory.

Rejected orders, cancellations, zero fills, settlements, redemptions, pending inventory, and incomplete exits add zero. Partial fills are combined into the same cycle.

RANK 1
Cash at qualifying crossing
Among bots that are flat at their first clean $1,000 crossing, the highest simulated cash wins.

RANK 2
Maximum drawdown
If cash ties exactly, the bot with lower peak-to-trough drawdown ranks higher.

RANK 3
Time to target
If both still tie, the bot reaching $1,000 sooner ranks higher.

Reality model

A midpoint backtest will not pass.
The paper evaluator models the expensive and awkward parts of real execution so results are useful to YeNo.

01
Pay both fee layers
The evaluator applies the 1% YeNo overlay observed in our test account and the dynamic crypto taker fee, 0.07 × shares × price × (1 − price), on entry and exit.

02
Trade the later book
Every action waits 250 ms, then crosses a later L2 update for its own outcome. An opposite-side update cannot reuse a pre-decision quote.

03
Expect partial fills
Displayed depth, minimum order size, split exits, share dust, and executable one-sided books near expiry are modeled. A partial exit remains the same cycle.

04
Treat writes as ambiguous
A timeout is not permission to retry. Reconcile the venue first or risk opening a duplicate position.

05
Respect the clock
Books older than two seconds are rejected. New entries are blocked inside the final 15 seconds. Qualification requires a flat account at the first clean $1,000 crossing.

06
Use causal reference data
Each request can include the public BTC midpoint, YeNo opening target, receive timestamps, and a provisional flag. Future-stamped target rows are excluded.

Protocol fee source: Polymarket's current fee documentation. It specifies the crypto rate, price curve, taker-only application, and five-decimal fee precision. The additional 1% overlay is modeled from observed YeNo test-account execution.

Market scope

BTC five-minute Up/Down only.
A narrow universe keeps market data, fills, and qualification comparable.

Hidden forward paper data
Qualification uses one unseen, receive-time-ordered 24-hour YeNo window. Internal recorded replay informed development but is not bundled with the starter kit or used for the official score.
